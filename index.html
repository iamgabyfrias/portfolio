Security Awareness

Corporate Security Awareness Training

Interactive eLearningAdult Professionals20–45 min

Challenge

A corporate client in finance and accounting was consistently failing phishing simulations, particularly those targeting their industry or appearing to come from "HR." Employees were also downloading malware-laden apps and clicking attachments from compromised contacts.

Solution

Designed a scenario-based eLearning experience with realistic audio examples, decision branches, and immediate feedback. Used storytelling to build emotional connection while teaching practical recognition skills.

Outcome

40.8%→3.4%

Phish-prone rate over six months of monthly simulations and targeted follow-up, showing measurable behavior change in a high-stakes environment.

AI Literacy

AI Literacy Curriculum

K–12Grades 3–510K+ global learners

Challenge

How do you teach machine-learning concepts to eight-year-olds? Traditional tech education was too abstract for young learners who need concrete, hands-on experiences.

Solution

Created "Strike a Pose," a project where students train an AI model to recognize emotions through body language, paired with video tutorials, worksheets, and collaborative reflection.

Outcome

Reached 10,000+ learners worldwide through Girls Who Code, with a replicable framework that let non-technical teachers facilitate real AI literacy, including honest discussion of AI bias and accuracy.

Professional Development

Technical Education Professional Development

Educator Training1 month12 educators

Challenge

High-school teachers with no technical background needed to confidently teach cybersecurity, IT support, and networking. Traditional professional development often fails to connect technical theory to real work.

Solution

Designed an experiential program with industry partnerships including Google and the Federal Reserve Bank of NY. Teachers experienced the concepts in real workplace settings, then adapted them for the classroom.

Outcome

Turned 12 educators with no technical background into confident instructors, and built 30+ lesson plans connecting cybersecurity, IT, and networking to career pathways in underserved communities.

ELA Curriculum

Global Human Rights Education

International ELAGrades 7–816 teachers coached

Challenge

How do you help students in China connect with global human-rights issues far from their own reality, while making difficult history accessible and meaningful?

Solution

Built a curriculum exploring children's rights through multiple perspectives, Darfur, the Holocaust, the Khmer Rouge, and U.S. segregation, using station-based learning with documentaries, music, literature, and cultural parallels.

Outcome

Students built global awareness, empathy, and critical thinking, and the framework was adopted across the school's humanities curriculum, with 16 teachers coached to deliver it.
